# Regulatory Governance Framework: EU AI Act (Article 50) & Editorial Control

## Legal Anchor: Article 50 Transparency Obligations
Under Article 50(4) of the European Union Artificial Intelligence Act, deployers of AI systems that generate or manipulate text published to inform the public on matters of public interest must disclose that the content has been artificially generated or manipulated.

### The "Human Review / Editorial Control" Exception
Article 50 provides a critical exemption: **disclosure is not required where the AI-generated content has undergone a process of human review or editorial control, and where a natural or legal person holds editorial responsibility for the publication of the content.**

## The Gatekeeper Crisis: Commercial "AI Humanizers"
The emergence of adversarial paraphrasing tools ("AI humanizers") threatens to undermine Article 50 compliance in two ways:
1. **Evading Automated Audits:** Humanizers deliberately alter token probabilities and burstiness to fool statistical watermarks, allowing unverified Stage 1 or Stage 2 copy to pass as Stage 5 human journalism.
2. **Eroding Provable Responsibility:** Without standardized linguistic logging, media organizations cannot empirically prove that "Active Editorial Control" occurred before publication.

## Operationalizing Compliance via the Five-Stage Taxonomy
This framework maps editorial workflows directly to regulatory accountability:

| Provenance Stage | Level of AI Intervention | Article 50 Disclosure Status | Gatekeeper Verification Requirement |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Stage 1** | 100% Synthetic | **Mandatory Disclosure** | Reject from standard news streams; label explicitly if published. |
| **Stage 2** | Heavy Prose Rewrite | **Conditional / High Risk** | Requires line-by-line factual verification and senior editor sign-off. |
| **Stage 3** | Light Proofreading | **Exempt (Editorial Control)** | Standard copy-editing audit trail. |
| **Stage 4** | Cross-Lingual / Idiom Aid | **Exempt (Editorial Control)** | Human author retains full narrative and semantic responsibility. |
| **Stage 5** | Zero AI Assistance | **Exempt (Organic Human)** | Baseline standard for investigative and opinion journalism. |
